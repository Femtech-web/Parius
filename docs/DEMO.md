# Parius demo brief

Use this brief with Claude to make a **2–3 minute** motion demo. Build it around one freelancer/client story, then show the real product completing that story. Keep the existing Satoshi typography, dark background, pale blue accents and generous spacing.

## The opening

> A client asks for an extra feature. The work starts, but the price stays the same. Later, a promise to pay gets buried between feedback and files.
>
> Parius keeps the conversation, agreement and payments together, so both people can see what changed and what is still owed. The freelancer can check a new request against the approved scope, prepare a payment follow-up and confirm useful lessons for a later review.

Use a short, illustrative message such as “Could we add a shopping cart and checkout?” Make the original agreement’s e-commerce exclusion visible. Avoid inventing a customer testimonial or presenting the research anecdote as a result achieved by Parius.

If the video needs to be shorter, trim transitions and setup. Preserve the learning evidence and the payment verification; they substantiate the product.

## Claims to keep accurate

The built product supports scope review, confirmed lessons, payment promises, reviewed follow-up drafts, invoice receipts, controlled file handoff, scoped MCP reads and one reviewed Sui MPP payment flow.

It does not continuously monitor WhatsApp/email, automatically detect every unbilled deliverable, automatically send reminders, learn reminder style from payment history, provide fiat cash-out, hold funds in escrow or resolve legal disputes. These research needs explain the wider problem; they are not all implemented features.

The supplied agent payment runner supports Testnet. It is an MPP implementation, not x402. Do not describe it as an unrestricted autonomous agent or a Mainnet payment demo.
